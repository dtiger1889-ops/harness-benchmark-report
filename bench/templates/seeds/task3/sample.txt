The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The dog barked at the fox.
The fox ran away quickly. A quick fox is a clever fox. The lazy dog slept.

are often clear and readable. Readability counts. Simple is better than
complex. Complex is better than complicated. Flat is better than nested.

often writes better code. Code is read more than it is written. The fox
agrees that readable code is the best code. The dog prefers sleeping.
